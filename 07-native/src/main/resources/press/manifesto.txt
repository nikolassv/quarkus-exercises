THE WANNEEIKEL MANIFESTO
=========================

We, the dreamers of Wanneeikel, hold these truths to be self-evident:

  1. That Free Love is the natural state of the awakened heart.
  2. That Peace is not the absence of noise, but the presence of the Rhodes piano.
  3. That Fusion Jazz, in 7/8, is the sound the universe makes when it stops worrying.

We renounce the time clock. We renounce the tie. We renounce the casserole made
without love.

Bring your own tambourine. The goat is already enlightened.

— The Editorial Collective, behind the supermarket, Spring 1972
